
📌 Overview

The Deposit Analysis Dashboard provides an interactive visual analysis of customer subscription behavior for term deposits. It helps users explore how different demographic and financial factors influence whether a customer subscribes to a deposit product.

This dashboard is designed to support business analysts, marketing teams, and decision-makers in identifying trends and optimizing campaign strategies.

🎯 Objectives

Analyze customer responses to deposit subscription campaigns.

Identify patterns across demographic and financial attributes.

Support data-driven marketing and customer targeting strategies.

Visualize key performance indicators in a clear and interactive way.

📊 Dashboard Components
1️⃣ Count of Deposit

Displays the total number of customers who:

Subscribed to the deposit (yes)

Did not subscribe (no)

This provides an overall success rate of the campaign.

2️⃣ Count of Deposit by Age

Shows distribution of deposit subscriptions across different age groups (binned ages).

Purpose:

Identify which age segments are more likely to subscribe.

Detect high-performing demographic groups.

3️⃣ Count of Deposit by Job

Donut chart visualizing deposit counts across job categories such as:

Management

Blue-collar

Technician

Admin

Services

Retired

Self-employed

Student

Purpose:

Understand which professions respond more positively.

Support profession-based targeting.

4️⃣ Count of Deposit by Loan

Shows deposit subscription distribution based on whether customers have a personal loan (yes / no).

Purpose:

Analyze how existing loan obligations influence deposit decisions.

5️⃣ Count of Deposit by Balance

Bar chart displaying customer balance ranges and their deposit subscription count.

Purpose:

Identify whether higher account balances correlate with higher subscription rates.

Detect financial capacity patterns.

6️⃣ Count of Deposit by Month

Displays subscription distribution across months.

Purpose:

Identify seasonal trends.

Determine high-performing campaign months.

🔎 Filters Available

Deposit: Yes / No

Contact Type: Cellular / Telephone / Unknown

These filters allow dynamic exploration of the dataset and focused analysis.
